## The 4 hospitals, compared

### 1. Bangkok Hospital: best for knowing exactly what you're paying for

**Plastic surgery at Bangkok Hospital headquarters · Huai Khwang · packages valid to 31 December 2026**

Bangkok Hospital publishes fly-in packages for the most common procedures, and each one states what's included and what isn't. Rhinoplasty under local anaesthetic is ฿46,000 as an outpatient. Reduction rhinoplasty is ฿207,000, or ฿265,000 with septum work, with one night in hospital. Breast augmentation is ฿190,000 (gel or saline) or ฿202,000 (endoscopic), with one night. Upper or lower eyelid surgery is ฿60,000, or ฿98,000 for both, as an outpatient.

Each Bangkok Hospital package covers the operating room, the surgeon and anaesthetist, a standard room, and the medication and supplies for the operation.

**Worth knowing:** Bangkok Hospital packages **exclude the implants themselves**, lab tests, pathology, other specialist consultations and treatment of complications. A breast augmentation quote can rise significantly once implants are added, so ask for the implant price in writing. The prices are for fly-in patients and can change without notice. The JCI period shown on Bangkok Hospital's site ended in February 2026, so ask for the current certificate.

**Best for:** patients who want to see the inclusions and exclusions before they book.

### 2. Yanhee International Hospital: best for the lowest published prices

**Dedicated cosmetic surgery hospital · Bang Phlat (Charansanitwong Soi 90)**

Yanhee is one of Bangkok's best-known cosmetic surgery hospitals, and it publishes the lowest from-prices of the four. Nose implants start at ฿30,000 (standard), ฿40,000 (USA implant) or ฿50,000 (Korean implant). Nose reshaping is ฿105,000 with two nights in hospital, and a rib-graft rhinoplasty is ฿220,000. Breast augmentation is ฿125,000 with Mentor round implants under 400cc, or ฿217,000 with Motiva Ergonomix, both with one night. Upper or lower eyelid surgery is ฿33,500 for women and ฿37,500 for men.

**Worth knowing:** Yanhee's prices are from-prices with no stated inclusions or validity date. The final price is set after a doctor's consultation. Yanhee's own accreditation page shows a JCI period that ended on 10 March 2026, so ask for the current certificate. It's on the Thonburi side of the river, away from the Sukhumvit hotels.

**Best for:** patients comparing price first, who are happy to confirm the details in a consultation.

### 3. Bumrungrad International Hospital: best for patients with other medical conditions

**Large international hospital · Watthana (Sukhumvit Soi 3) · JCI accredited since 2002**

Bumrungrad was the first hospital in Asia to gain JCI accreditation, in 2002. Its current certificate runs to 1 December 2026. The main reason to choose Bumrungrad for cosmetic surgery is everything around the operation: if you have a heart condition, diabetes or another health issue, every specialty is in the same building.

**Worth knowing:** Bumrungrad doesn't publish plastic surgery prices on its website, so you can't compare costs before you ask. Expect the highest prices of the four, and get an itemised quote that includes implants and follow-up.

**Best for:** patients whose medical history makes hospital backup a priority.

### 4. Kamol Cosmetic Hospital: best for a cosmetic-only hospital with named surgeons

**Dedicated cosmetic surgery hospital · Bangkok**

Kamol Cosmetic Hospital does cosmetic surgery and nothing else. It names seven plastic surgeons on its website, led by its chief surgeon, Dr Kamol Pansritum, so you can check each one with the Medical Council of Thailand before you book. Kamol states that it holds JCI accreditation.

**Worth knowing:** Kamol doesn't publish prices or the dates of its accreditation. Ask for both, in writing, before you commit.

**Best for:** patients who want to choose their surgeon by name at a cosmetic-only hospital.

## Hospital or clinic for cosmetic surgery?

A **cosmetic surgery hospital** (Yanhee, Kamol) does cosmetic work and nothing else. Prices tend to be lower than at a general hospital, and the teams do these operations every day.

A **general private hospital** (Bangkok Hospital, Bumrungrad) adds every other specialty and an intensive care unit on site. A general hospital is the safer choice if you have a significant medical condition. You'll pay more for it.

For any operation under general anaesthesia, check that the place is licensed as a hospital or a licensed surgical facility, not just a clinic. A beauty clinic that also does injectables is not the same thing.

## Questions to send every hospital

1. Who is my surgeon, and what is their Medical Council of Thailand licence number?

5. Who pays if there's a complication or I need a revision?

## FAQ

### What is the best hospital in Bangkok for cosmetic surgery?

It depends on your procedure, budget and health. Bangkok Hospital publishes the clearest packages, Yanhee publishes the lowest prices, Bumrungrad suits patients with other medical conditions, and Kamol is a cosmetic-only hospital with named surgeons. Shortlist two and compare written quotes.

### How much does a nose job cost in Bangkok?

Published rhinoplasty prices in Bangkok in October 2026 run from ฿30,000 for a basic nose implant at Yanhee to ฿265,000 for reduction rhinoplasty with septum work at Bangkok Hospital. The price depends on the technique, the implant or graft, and the number of nights in hospital.

### How much is breast augmentation in Bangkok?

Yanhee publishes ฿125,000–217,000 including the implant, depending on the brand. Bangkok Hospital publishes ฿190,000–202,000, but that excludes the implants themselves. Always ask which implant brand and size the price includes.

### Is Yanhee Hospital JCI accredited?

Yanhee's own accreditation page shows a JCI period from 11 March 2023 to 10 March 2026. As of October 2026 it did not show a renewed period, so ask Yanhee for its current certificate before you book.

### How long should I stay in Bangkok after cosmetic surgery?

It depends on the operation and on your surgeon's advice. The hospitals publish only the nights you spend in hospital (none to two for most procedures). Ask your surgeon how long you should stay in Bangkok before flying, and plan for follow-up visits and stitch removal.
