## What is dermal filler, and what differs between clinics?

Dermal filler is a gel injected under the skin to replace volume. Almost all of it in Bangkok is hyaluronic acid, a sugar your body already makes, which means it can be dissolved if something goes wrong. That single property is why HA filler is the sensible default and why permanent fillers are not.

It does a different job from Botox. Botox stops a muscle pulling; filler puts back what has gone. Sunken cheeks, undereye shadows, thin lips, a receding chin and a soft jawline are volume problems, and no amount of toxin fixes them.

The brand decides the price, and the brands are not interchangeable:

| Brand | Made by | Origin | Typical position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Juvederm** | Allergan | USA | The most recognised range; Volux and Voluma for structure, Volbella for lips |
| **Restylane** | Galderma | Sweden | Equally established; Kysse for lips, Lyft for cheeks |
| **Belotero** | Merz | Switzerland | Soft and even; often chosen for fine lines and undereye |
| **Teoxane RHA** | Teoxane | Switzerland | Designed to move naturally in animated areas |
| **Neuramis / Yvoire** | Medytox / LG | South Korea | Half the price of Western HA; shorter longevity |
| **Sculptra** | Galderma | France | Not a filler. Collagen biostimulator, gradual, lasts around two years |
| **HArmonyCa** | Allergan | Ireland | Hybrid, HA plus calcium hydroxyapatite. Not dissolvable in the same way |

Filler lasts roughly 8 to 18 months depending on the product, the area and how much you move it. Lips go fastest, structural jaw and chin filler lasts longest.

## What can dermal filler help with?

- **Restoring lost volume** in cheeks and temples, which is what makes a face read as tired rather than lined.
- **Softening static folds**, particularly nasolabial folds and marionette lines that stay visible when your face is still.
- **Shaping**, a stronger chin, a defined jawline, fuller lips, a straighter nose bridge without surgery.
- **Undereye hollows**, the single most technically demanding area and the one to be most careful about.

If your problem is loose skin rather than lost volume, filler is the wrong tool and will make a heavy face heavier. Lifting treatments answer that instead.

## Which areas can dermal filler treat?

| Area | Typical volume | What it does | Risk level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheeks | 1–2 cc | Restores mid-face volume | Moderate |
| Nasolabial folds | 1 cc | Softens the fold nose to mouth | Moderate |
| Lips | 0.5–1 cc | Adds volume and definition | Moderate |
| Chin | 1–2 cc | Projects a receding chin | Moderate |
| Jawline | 2–4 cc | Defines and sharpens | Moderate |
| Temples | 1–2 cc | Fills hollowing at the sides | **High** |
| Undereye / tear trough | 0.5–1 cc | Softens shadows and hollows | **High** |
| Nose | 0.5–1 cc | Straightens the bridge | **Highest** |

The high-risk areas are high-risk for one reason: major blood vessels run through them. Filler injected into an artery can block it, and in the nose, undereye and temple that can mean skin loss or, very rarely, permanent loss of vision. These are not areas to choose on price.

## How to choose the best dermal filler clinic in Bangkok

| Factor | What to confirm before you book |
|---|---|
| **Hyaluronidase on site** | The enzyme that dissolves HA filler. It is the emergency treatment for a blocked vessel and it has to be in the building, not ordered in. Ask directly. Any good clinic answers instantly. |
| **The brand and the volume** | Named brand, and cc, in writing. "Cheek filler package" tells you nothing about how much gel you are getting. |
| **The syringe opened in front of you** | Sealed, branded, in date. Counterfeit HA is the real risk at the cheap end. |
| **A doctor injecting** | Filler complications are vascular emergencies. You want someone who can recognise and treat one. |
| **HA, not permanent filler** | Silicone and other permanent fillers cannot be dissolved and cause problems years later. Decline them. |
| **Conservative first treatment** | Good injectors under-fill and review. Filler can be added in two weeks; removing it means dissolving the lot and starting again. |

## Is filler good no matter where you get it?

No, and the stakes are higher than with Botox. Botox wears off in three to four months; a badly placed filler sits in your face for a year or more, and correcting it means dissolving everything and starting again. Migrated lip filler and overfilled cheeks are the two most common regrets, and both come from injector judgement rather than from the product.

The serious risk is vascular. Filler injected into or against an artery can block the blood supply, causing skin necrosis and, rarely, permanent vision loss. It is uncommon, it is a genuine emergency, and it is treatable with hyaluronidase if the clinic has it on hand and recognises what is happening within hours. That is why the question about hyaluronidase is not paranoid and should never embarrass you to ask.

## Why should a doctor perform dermal filler?

Because the entire skill is knowing what is under the needle. The facial artery, the angular artery and the supratrochlear artery run through the exact areas people most want treated, and their position varies from face to face. A doctor knows the anatomy, injects in the safer plane, aspirates or uses a cannula where it matters, and can recognise the early signs of an occlusion, blanching, disproportionate pain, dusky skin, and act immediately.

A doctor is also the person who should decline. Filler in a face that needs lifting makes it heavier. Filler over migrated filler compounds a problem. Undereye filler in someone with poor skin quality or fluid retention looks worse, not better. A clinic that will inject anything you ask for is not assessing you.

## FAQ

### How much does dermal filler cost in Bangkok?

Published prices run from about ฿5,980 per cc for Korean HA fillers such as Neuramis up to ฿21,000 per cc for premium Juvederm at the more expensive clinics. Established Western brands typically sit between ฿9,900 and ฿18,000 per cc. Most areas need one to two cc; a jawline can need four. Biostimulators like Sculptra are priced differently again, from about ฿26,980.

### Which filler brand is best in Bangkok?

There is no single best, because different products do different jobs. Juvederm Volux and Restylane Lyft are firm and structural, for jaw and cheeks. Volbella and Kysse are soft, for lips. Belotero and Teoxane RHA are chosen for fine lines and animated areas. Korean brands such as Neuramis and Yvoire cost roughly half and last a little less. What matters more than the brand is that it is named, sealed and hyaluronic acid.

### Is dermal filler safe?

HA filler is generally safe in trained hands, and its key advantage is that it can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if you dislike the result or something goes wrong. The serious risk is filler entering a blood vessel, which can cause skin damage and, very rarely, vision loss. It is uncommon but it is an emergency, so only use a clinic where a doctor injects and hyaluronidase is kept on site.

### How long does dermal filler last?

Roughly 8 to 18 months, depending on the product and the area. Lips break down fastest because they move constantly, often 6 to 9 months. Structural filler in the chin and jaw lasts longest, sometimes beyond 18 months. Sculptra is different: it stimulates your own collagen and typically lasts around two years, but it builds gradually over months rather than showing a result immediately.

### Can dermal filler be removed?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an injection of hyaluronidase, usually within minutes to days. That is the main reason to insist on HA rather than a permanent filler. Biostimulators such as Sculptra and hybrids like HArmonyCa cannot be dissolved in the same straightforward way, which is worth knowing before you choose one.
