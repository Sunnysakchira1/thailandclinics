One cycle of IVF or ICSI in Thailand costs about ฿150,000–456,000 at the package prices Bangkok providers published in October 2026. Most couples should budget ฿250,000–450,000 before medication. Medication can add another ฿60,000–150,000, and genetic testing, embryo freezing and frozen transfers are often charged on top.

The spread is wide because packages cover different things. A ฿150,000 package and a ฿335,000 package aren't the same cycle, so compare what's inside before you compare the price. ## Why the price varies so much

**Your age and ovarian reserve.** Some packages depend on your age and AMH level. At GFC, women over 37 go straight to the ฿230,000 or ฿350,000 package. Ask which package you qualify for before you compare.

**Medication.** Most packages cap the stimulation dose. GFC's packages cap it at 2,400–4,000 IU. If you need more, you pay the difference. Bangkok Hospital's Package A lists ฿138,000 for stimulation, which covers 10 days of medication, 3 scans and 3 doctor visits.

**Genetic testing (PGT-A).** This is the biggest add-on. GFC charges ฿39,900 for the first embryo and ฿15,900 for each one after. Bangkok Hospital charges ฿96,000 for PGT-A plus freezing for 2 embryos. Not everyone needs PGT. Ask your doctor whether it helps in your case.

**Frozen embryo transfer.** If you freeze embryos and transfer later, the transfer is usually a separate bill. GFC lists a frozen transfer at ฿40,000.

**Hospital or dedicated clinic.** Hospital-based centres tend to cost more. You pay for every other specialty being on site, which matters if you have another medical condition. Our [comparison of IVF hospitals and centres in Bangkok](/blog/best-ivf-hospitals-bangkok/) explains when that's worth it.

**A second cycle.** Many people need more than one retrieval or transfer. Ask what a second attempt costs before you start the first.

## Who can have IVF in Thailand

Price only matters if you're eligible. Thailand's assisted reproduction law, the ART Act of 2015, limits IVF and ICSI to a legally married couple. Clinics ask for your marriage certificate. Foreign married couples can be treated. A certificate issued abroad usually needs a translation and an embassy or legalisation stamp, so check with the clinic early.

Single women can't have IVF in Thailand. Same-sex couples have been able to marry since 23 January 2025, but the ART Act still says "husband and wife", and clinics generally aren't treating married same-sex couples yet. A draft amendment that would change the wording to "spouses" went to public consultation in July 2026 but had not become law at the time of writing. Ask the clinic directly about your case. ## What to check before you pay

- **The exact inclusions.** Ask whether the package covers stimulation drugs, scans, doctor fees, anaesthesia, the lab, embryo culture and the transfer.
- **The medication cap.** Ask what the cap is in IU and what each extra unit costs.
- **Freezing and storage.** Ask what embryo freezing costs and what storage costs each year after that.
- **PGT pricing.** Ask whether it's priced per embryo or as a block, and how long results take.
- **Who the price is for.** Some packages are for residents only and some are for fly-in patients only.
- **Expiry dates.** Bangkok Hospital and Prime list prices valid to 31 December 2026.
- **Cancellation.** Ask what you pay if your cycle is cancelled or you get no usable eggs.

Insurance rarely covers fertility treatment, so most people pay out of pocket. If you plan to claim anything back, ask for itemised receipts.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest IVF in Thailand?

The lowest published price we found in October 2026 was GFC's LiTE ICSI package at ฿150,000. It's only for women aged 35 or under with an AMH of at least 1.2. If you don't meet those conditions, your package will cost more.

### Does the IVF price include medication?

Usually only up to a limit. Most Bangkok packages cover a set amount of stimulation medication, and you pay for anything above it. Budget ฿60,000–150,000 for medication if a quote leaves it out.

### How much does IVF with PGT cost in Bangkok?

Bangkok Hospital publishes ฿456,000 for a cycle with PGT-A and freezing for 2 embryos. At GFC you add ฿39,900 for the first tested embryo and ฿15,900 for each one after to the ICSI package price.

### Is IVF cheaper in Thailand than in the UK or US?

Usually yes, but compare like for like. Add flights, 2–4 weeks of accommodation and the chance of needing a second cycle. Bangkok Hospital tells fly-in patients to plan for about 3–4 weeks in Thailand for one full cycle.

### Can foreigners get the same IVF price as Thai patients?

Not always. MedPark's ฿280,000 package is for Thai nationals and expats living in Thailand only. Bangkok Hospital's packages are for fly-in patients only. Ask which price applies to you.
