A physio rehab retreat in Thailand is a resort stay with physiotherapy built into a wellness programme: Kamalaya on Koh Samui charges ฿105,568 for 8 nights, excluding accommodation, and 4 of its 13 treatments are with a physiotherapist. It suits mild, stable problems, but if you've just had surgery or need intensive rehab, a Bangkok physio clinic gives you more physio sessions for a fraction of the price.

**Updated 31 October 2026.** Programme details come from each resort's own website, where it publishes them. We haven't visited these retreats, and listing them isn't an endorsement.

## Retreats with physiotherapy on site

### Kamalaya, Koh Samui: Structural Revival

Kamalaya's Structural Revival programme runs for 5, 8, 11 or 15 nights. Kamalaya says it's for people who sit at desks for long periods and for rehabilitation after injury or surgery. The 8-night version (13 treatments) costs ฿105,568, excluding accommodation, at rates valid until 31 March 2027. It includes:

- a physiotherapy session with a 3D body scan
- revival exercise, myofascial release and aqua fitness, each with a physiotherapist
- Pilates, an osteopath session, traditional Thai therapeutic treatment, massage and hyperbaric oxygen therapy

### Amanpuri, Phuket: Performance and Recovery

Amanpuri describes its Performance and Recovery retreat as physiotherapy-based. Its sample three-day programme starts with a physical assessment with a physiotherapist and/or a wellness consultation with a doctor. It combines therapeutic massage, training sessions, hydrotherapy and acupuncture. Amanpuri doesn't publish a price; you ask its wellness team.

### STAY Wellbeing and Lifestyle Resort, Phuket: STAY WELL Clinic and Physio

STAY, in Rawai, has an on-site clinic, STAY WELL Clinic and Physio, covering physiotherapy, cryotherapy, anti-ageing treatments and longevity medicine. We couldn't find its rehab programmes or prices on the resort's own website, so ask the resort what a physio programme includes and who leads it.

The retreat price buys more than physio: meals, the setting and the other treatments. Those are worth paying for if you want them. But if rehab is the goal, count the physio sessions, not the total treatments.

## Who a retreat suits, and who it doesn't

**A retreat can work if** you have a stable, long-running problem such as desk-related neck and back pain, you've been cleared by your doctor, and you want rest alongside treatment.

**A clinic or hospital is the better choice if** you're in the first weeks after surgery, need physio most days, or need a doctor nearby. After ACL reconstruction, the AAOS says most patients return to full sport within 6–12 months, so a week away is one part of the recovery, not all of it. For stroke or complex surgery, a hospital rehabilitation centre has equipment and specialists a resort doesn't.

## What to check before you book

- **Who leads your rehab?** It should be a physiotherapist licensed by the Physical Therapy Council of Thailand. Ask for a name and licence number.
- **How many sessions are with a physio?** Count them in the programme. Massage, Pilates and fitness classes are different things.
- **Has your surgeon cleared you?** Send the retreat your operation notes and rehab protocol, and ask your surgeon before any pool work.
- **Where's the nearest hospital?** Islands like Koh Samui and Phuket are a flight or a long drive from Bangkok's specialist centres.
- **Will insurance pay?** Some insurers only cover physio a doctor has referred you for. Allianz Care's benefit guide, for example, excludes massage and Pilates from its physio cover. Don't assume a retreat is covered until your insurer confirms it.
- **What happens when you leave?** Ask for a written home programme and a progress summary for your physio at home.

## FAQ

### Is a rehab retreat in Thailand worth it?

It can be for stable, long-running problems when you also want rest. For rehab after recent surgery, a clinic usually gives you more physiotherapy for less. Count the physio sessions in any programme before you compare prices.

### Does insurance cover a physio retreat?

Don't count on it. Insurers pay for physiotherapy under their own rules, and some only after a doctor's referral. Allianz Care's Flexicare plan, for example, requires a doctor's referral for its prescribed physio benefit and excludes massage and Pilates. Ask your insurer before you book.

### Can I do a rehab retreat straight after surgery?

Ask your surgeon first. Early rehab follows your surgeon's protocol, and if something goes wrong you may need a doctor quickly. Ask whether a city clinic near a hospital would be the safer choice at that stage.

### Is "rehab in Thailand" the same as addiction rehab?

No. Several Thai "rehab" centres, such as Hope Rehab in Si Racha, treat addiction. Physical rehab means physiotherapy for injury, surgery or pain. Check which one a centre offers before you enquire.
