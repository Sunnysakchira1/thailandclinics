## Why prices vary so much

**Session length.** Form Recovery and Wellness and BPC Physio both sell 60, 90 and 120-minute sessions. A 90-minute session costs ฿2,900 at both, so a longer first visit can cost more than an hour of follow-up.

**The consultation.** Form Recovery and Wellness charges ฿1,000 for a 30-minute first consultation, BPC Physio charges ฿700 and Pain Away Clinic ฿500. Pain Away says a consultation is required before any first treatment. Ask whether the consultation is separate from your first treatment.

**Doctors' fees.** Hospitals may start you with a doctor. Bumrungrad says its rehabilitation medicine specialists carry out the diagnosis and plan your treatment. Expect to pay for that doctor's time on top of the physio session.

**Specialist add-ons.** BPC Physio charges ฿3,300 for the first assessment on its ACL Rehab Course (105 minutes) and ฿1,000 for VALD strength testing. Form Recovery and Wellness charges ฿2,100 for 60 minutes of aqua therapy.

## Will insurance pay?

It depends on your policy, so read the wording before your first session. Some international health plans only pay for physio a doctor has referred you for. Allianz Care's Flexicare benefit guide (valid from 1 June 2026), for example, defines its prescribed physiotherapy as treatment by a registered physiotherapist "following referral by a doctor". It limits physio to 12 sessions per condition before your therapist or referring doctor must review your treatment, and says physiotherapy doesn't include massage or Pilates.

## What to check before you pay

- **Is the price per session or per hour?** And how much of that time is hands-on with a physiotherapist?
- **Is there a doctor's fee?** Hospitals and some clinics add one.
- **Is your physiotherapist licensed?** Physiotherapists in Thailand must be licensed by the Physical Therapy Council. Ask for a name and licence number.
- **Does your insurer need a referral?** Get it before the first session, not after.
- **Is a package refundable?** Check before you prepay for sessions you may not use.

## FAQ

### How much is one physiotherapy session in Bangkok?

In October 2026, a 60-minute session cost ฿2,000 at BPC Physio and ฿2,100 at Form Recovery and Wellness. Pain Away Clinic charged ฿2,090 for 60 minutes of manual treatment. The six Bangkok hospitals we checked don't publish physio prices, and you may pay a doctor's fee on top.

### Do I have to pay for a consultation first?

At some clinics, yes. Form Recovery and Wellness charges ฿1,000 and BPC Physio ฿700 for a 30-minute first consultation, and Pain Away Clinic charges ฿500. Ask whether it includes any treatment.

### Is hospital physiotherapy more expensive?

You can't easily tell, because none of the six Bangkok hospitals we checked in October 2026 published a physio price. You may see a rehabilitation doctor first, which adds a fee. Ask for an itemised quote before you book.

### Does insurance cover physiotherapy in Thailand?

Some international health plans cover physio, but the conditions vary. Some only pay if a doctor referred you, cap the number of sessions or exclude massage. Check your policy wording and ask about pre-authorisation before you start.
