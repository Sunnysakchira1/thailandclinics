## When to go to a hospital first

The NHS says to go to an emergency department after an injury if:

- you heard a crack, or the injured part has changed shape
- the area is numb or tingling, or the skin has changed colour or feels cold

The NHS also says to get urgent medical advice if you can't put weight on it, the pain or swelling is getting worse, or you have a high temperature. If a fracture is possible, start at a hospital, where you can be X-rayed.

## How to choose a sports injury clinic

- **A licensed physiotherapist.** Physiotherapists in Thailand must be licensed by the Physical Therapy Council. Ask for a name and licence number.
- **Experience with your injury.** Ask how many ACL, ankle or tendon cases they see.
- **Measured progress.** Strength testing shows whether the injured side has caught up. That matters most before you return to sport.
- **Return-to-sport criteria.** After ACL reconstruction, the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS) says most patients return to full sport within 6–12 months, depending on their progress, strength and movement. Ask how the clinic will decide you're ready.
- **A published price.** BPC Physio and Form Recovery and Wellness publish prices. The hospitals below don't, and may add a doctor's fee.
- **The same physiotherapist each visit.** Ask for it when you book.

## 5 sports injury options in Bangkok

The order is ThailandClinics' editorial assessment for a typical sports injury. Clinics come first because you can start most sprains, strains and overuse injuries with a physio, unless the hospital signs above apply. It isn't a ranking of treatment outcomes.

### 1. BPC Physio: for sports injuries and ACL rehab

[BPC Physio](/bangkok/physiotherapy-clinics/bpc-physio/) is a physio-only clinic with branches in Sukhumvit 39, Sathorn and Sanam Pao. It charges ฿700 for a 30-minute consultation and ฿2,000 for 60 minutes. Its ACL Rehab Course starts with a ฿3,300 assessment (105 minutes), and VALD strength testing costs ฿1,000. Reviewers describe progress after ACL, meniscus and ankle surgery.

**Worth knowing:** two reviewers felt pushed towards twice-weekly sessions, and a few say quality depends on which physio you see.

### 2. Form Recovery and Wellness: for phased rehab after surgery

[Form Recovery and Wellness](/bangkok/physiotherapy-clinics/form-recovery-and-wellness-at-asoke/) has branches in Asoke, Thonglor and Phrom Phong. A 30-minute consultation is ฿1,000 and 60 minutes of physio is ฿2,100. Each branch has gym space, and the clinic publishes a six-phase ACL reconstruction programme that moves you on by milestones rather than dates.

**Worth knowing:** you pay per session, so ask how many sessions your rehab phase is likely to need before you buy a package.

### 3. Unique Care Station (Rama 9): for tendon and soft-tissue injuries

[Unique Care Station](/bangkok/physiotherapy-clinics/unique-care-station-param-9/) in Huai Khwang focuses on Achilles pain, plantar fasciitis and long-standing tendon problems, using shockwave and ultrasound. Reviewers with long-standing sports injuries describe the team tracking progress across sessions.

**Worth knowing:** we haven't verified its prices. Ask for the session price and length when you book.

### 4. Samitivej Sukhumvit Sports and Orthopedic Center: for a doctor-led diagnosis

Samitivej's Sports and Orthopedic Center, on the first floor of Building 1 at Samitivej Sukhumvit Hospital, brings together orthopaedic surgeons, physical therapists and sports injury and rehabilitation specialists. It lists isokinetic rehab equipment, balance assessment and VO2 max testing, and treats ACL injuries, meniscus tears, rotator cuff problems and ankle sprains.

**Worth knowing:** you'll pay hospital rates, and physio prices aren't published.

### 5. Bangkok Hospital BASEM: if you may need surgery

The Bangkok Academy of Sports and Exercise Medicine (BASEM) is on the fifth floor of Building D at Bangkok Hospital. It treats sports injuries to bones, joints, tendons, ligaments, meniscus and cartilage, with or without keyhole (arthroscopic) surgery.

**Worth knowing:** BASEM is doctor-led and doesn't publish physio prices. Ask for an itemised quote, and ask where your longer rehab will happen.

## FAQ

### Do I need a referral to see a sports physio in Bangkok?

No. World Physiotherapy reports that people in Thailand can refer themselves to a physiotherapist, in private practice and in the public system. Hospitals may want you to see a doctor first, and some insurers only pay for physio a doctor has referred you for.

### How much does sports injury physio cost in Bangkok?

In October 2026, 60 minutes cost ฿2,000 at BPC Physio and ฿2,100 at Form Recovery and Wellness, with first consultations of ฿700 and ฿1,000. The two hospital sports medicine centres above don't publish physio prices.

### Should I see a doctor or a physio first?

For many strains, sprains and overuse injuries, you can start with a physio, who should refer you on if needed. See a doctor first if a fracture is possible, you can't put weight on it, or you have numbness, tingling or a high temperature.

### How long until I can play again after an ACL injury?

After ACL reconstruction, the AAOS says most patients return to full sport within 6–12 months, once strength, function and balance in the leg are fully restored. Your surgeon and physio will set your own timeline.
