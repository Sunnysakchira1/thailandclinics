## What is the Ulthera Prime device? What to know before choosing a clinic

Ulthera Prime is Merz Aesthetics' fourth-generation Ultherapy system. The energy is the same micro-focused ultrasound that Ultherapy has always used, delivered at three fixed depths (1.5mm, 3.0mm and 4.5mm), with the deepest passing through the skin to heat the SMAS, the fibrous layer a surgeon tightens in a facelift. Heating it triggers new collagen over the following months. Nothing is cut and nothing is injected.

What changed with Prime is the hardware, not the biology. Merz lists four upgrades:

- A **smaller, lighter handpiece and body**, which makes it easier for a doctor to hold a steady angle on curved areas like the jawline.
- A **35% larger scanning screen**, so more tissue is visible in one view before energy is fired.
- **Around 20% faster treatment**, which is the difference between a 90-minute session and a 70-minute one on a full face and neck.
- **More even energy delivery** and better treatment planning, from the See-Plan-Treat imaging that shows the doctor the actual layers under the probe in real time.

That imaging is the part worth caring about. It is what separates Ultherapy from generic HIFU devices, which fire blind. It only helps if the person holding the probe reads it and adjusts, which is why the clinic matters more than the machine.

## What can Ulthera Prime help with?

- **Lifting and firming lax skin** on the lower face, jawline and neck, the sagging that starts to show in your late thirties and forties.
- **Softening fine wrinkles and crepey texture**, particularly on the neck and décolletage.
- **Sharpening facial contours**, a more defined jawline and less heaviness under the chin.
- **Stimulating collagen** for gradual firming that keeps developing for two to three months after the session.

It is a lifting treatment, not a volume treatment. If the problem is hollowness in the cheeks or temples, filler or a biostimulator like Sculptra answers that better, and a good clinic will say so.

## Is Ulthera Prime good no matter where you get it?

No. The device is the same everywhere; the result is not. Ulthera Prime shows the doctor a live ultrasound image of the layers under the probe, and the entire advantage of the system is a clinician who reads that image and adjusts depth and placement as they go. Two doctors with identical machines and identical line counts can produce clearly different results on the same face. You are paying for the reading, not the firing.

## Why should a doctor perform Ulthera Prime?

Because the decisions are medical ones. A doctor has to judge how lax your skin actually is: ultrasound lifting works on mild to moderate laxity and disappoints on severe sagging, which needs threads or surgery. They have to map where your facial nerve branches run and avoid firing over them, work around any filler, threads or implants already in your face, and set a line count that matches the anatomy rather than the price list. Get those wrong and the outcomes are not just underwhelming: prolonged nerve pain, temporary weakness on one side of the face, and visible fat loss where energy went too deep are all documented. Ask who is holding the probe, and ask before you pay.

### Is Ulthera Prime better than regular Ultherapy?

The treatment is the same. Prime is the newer machine: a bigger scanning screen, a lighter handpiece, more even energy delivery and around 20% faster sessions. The ultrasound energy, the three treatment depths and the SMAS target are identical to Ulthera SPT. SPT costs roughly 30% less and remains a reasonable choice, particularly on a straightforward lower face.

### How long do Ulthera Prime results last?

Most people see visible firming two to three months after the session as new collagen forms, with results generally holding for around a year. Because it works by stimulating your own collagen, longevity depends on your age and skin condition. Many patients repeat it annually, or top up a single area more often.

### Does Ulthera Prime hurt?

You will feel heat and brief deep prickling, strongest along the jawline and over bone. Clinics apply numbing cream beforehand and use cooling gel during the session. Prime's faster delivery shortens the uncomfortable part rather than removing it. Most people describe it as tolerable and return for repeat treatments.

### Can I get Ulthera Prime if I already have filler or threads?

Usually yes, but tell the clinic exactly what you have had and when. Focused ultrasound heats the tissue where filler and threads sit, so the doctor needs to plan around them. This is one of the clearest reasons to have a doctor rather than a technician perform the treatment.
