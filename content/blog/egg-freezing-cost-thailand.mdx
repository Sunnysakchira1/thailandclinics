One egg-freezing cycle in Thailand costs about ฿120,000–265,000 at the prices Bangkok providers published in October 2026. Most packages include the first one to three years of storage. After that, budget for a yearly storage fee, which runs from about ฿1,300 a vial at Bangkok Hospital up to ฿10,000–30,000 a year at other clinics.

The range is wide because some packages include medication and some don't. Compare what's inside before you compare the price. To see providers side by side, start with our directory of [fertility clinics in Bangkok](/bangkok/fertility-clinics/).

## Why the price varies

**Medication.** This is the biggest swing. Bangkok Hospital and Nakornthon include a set amount of stimulation drugs. MedPark's package excludes medication, so its ฿180,000 isn't comparable to a package that includes it. If you need a higher dose, you pay the difference.

**How many eggs are frozen.** Nakornthon includes the first 10 eggs, GFC up to 20, and MedPark has no cap. If you produce a lot of eggs, a capped package can cost more.

**Storage.** Packages include one to three years. After that you pay each year, either per vial or as a flat fee. Ask what year four costs before you sign.

**Number of cycles.** Many women need more than one retrieval to bank enough eggs. MedPark suggests 8–10 eggs per hoped-for pregnancy if you're under 35. Nakornthon says 15–20 eggs gives a better chance of one pregnancy. Your doctor will give you a target based on your age and AMH level.

**Where you live.** Some prices are for residents only, some for fly-in patients only. Ask which price applies to you.

## The legal points that affect cost

Egg freezing is available to single women at Bangkok clinics. GFC's page lists it as suited to single women, and to women delaying pregnancy or facing treatment that could harm their ovaries.

Using the eggs later is different. Fertilising them is IVF, and under Thailand's ART Act of 2015, IVF is limited to legally married couples. The current law also bans exporting eggs from Thailand. A draft amendment would allow it with committee approval, but it went to public consultation in July 2026 and hadn't become law at the time of writing. So plan on paying storage in Thailand and coming back here to use your eggs. Read more in [can single women do IVF in Thailand](/blog/ivf-single-women-thailand/).

## What to check before you pay

- **Is medication included?** If not, ask for an estimate in baht for your likely dose.
- **The egg cap.** Ask how many eggs the package freezes and what each extra one costs.
- **Storage after the package.** Get the yearly fee in writing, and ask whether it's per vial or flat.
- **Who the price is for.** Residents-only and fly-in-only prices aren't interchangeable.
- **Expiry dates.** Bangkok Hospital lists its package as valid to 31 December 2026.
- **A cancelled cycle.** Ask what you pay if the cycle is stopped or no eggs are retrieved.
- **Time in Bangkok.** GFC says a cycle takes about 2 weeks from the first injection to retrieval. Add that to your accommodation budget.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest egg freezing in Thailand?

The lowest published package we found in October 2026 was Nakornthon Hospital's at ฿169,000, which includes medication up to 2,400 units and a year's storage. GFC starts from ฿170,000. MedPark's ฿180,000 excludes medication.

### How much does it cost to store frozen eggs in Thailand?

Most packages include the first one to three years. After that, Bangkok Hospital charges ฿1,300 per vial a year. Other clinics typically charge ฿10,000–30,000 a year. Ask for the exact fee in writing.

### Can a single woman freeze her eggs in Thailand?

Yes. Bangkok clinics such as GFC offer egg freezing to single women. You can only use the eggs for IVF in Thailand once you're legally married.

### Can I take my frozen eggs home from Thailand?

Not under the current law, which bans exporting eggs. A 2026 draft amendment would allow it with committee approval, but it isn't law yet. Ask the clinic whether anything has changed before you start.

### What is the best age to freeze your eggs?

Clinics agree that younger is better. GFC calls 20–35 the ideal window, and Nakornthon recommends freezing before 35. Freezing is still possible after that, often with fewer eggs per cycle.
