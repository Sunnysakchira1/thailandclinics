## What is Botox, and what actually differs between clinics?

Botox is botulinum toxin type A, injected in tiny doses to relax a specific muscle. The muscle stops pulling, the skin over it stops creasing, and the line softens. It starts working in three to seven days, peaks at about two weeks, and wears off over three to four months.

"Botox" is a brand name, not the treatment. Bangkok clinics carry several toxins, and the price gap between them is the single largest variable in what you pay.

| Brand | Made by | Origin | Typical position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Botox (Allergan)** | AbbVie | USA | The original. Most expensive, longest track record |
| **Dysport** | Ipsen | UK/France | Spreads slightly wider; units are **not** comparable to Allergan |
| **Xeomin** | Merz | Germany | No complexing proteins, so less likely to build resistance |
| **Nabota** | Daewoong | South Korea | First Asian toxin with FDA approval; mid-price |
| **Neuronox / Botulax / Aestox** | Various | South Korea | Budget tier, widely used in Bangkok |

**The trap in per-unit pricing:** Dysport units are not the same size as Allergan units. It takes roughly two and a half Dysport units to do the work of one Allergan unit. A clinic quoting a low "per unit" price on Dysport is not necessarily cheaper. Compare the price for the *area*, or the price for a full vial, not the unit.

## What can Botox help with?

- **Softening dynamic lines**, the forehead, frown and crow's feet lines that appear when you move your face.
- **Slimming a square jaw** by relaxing the masseter muscles, which also helps if you grind your teeth.
- **Stopping excessive sweating** in the underarms, palms or feet, where results last longer than facial Botox, often six months or more.
- **Relaxing tight shoulders** by treating the trapezius, which is popular in Bangkok and lengthens the neckline.

It only works on lines caused by movement. If a line is etched in when your face is completely still, that is a static line, and filler or a resurfacing treatment will do more for it than Botox will.

## How to choose the best Botox clinic in Bangkok

| Factor | What to confirm before you book |
|---|---|
| **Brand and dose in writing** | The brand name and the number of units, not "one area". Per-area pricing hides how much toxin you are actually getting. |
| **The vial opened in front of you** | Counterfeit and re-diluted toxin is the real risk at the cheap end. Ask to see the sealed vial and its label. |
| **A doctor injecting** | Not a nurse, not a therapist. Facial anatomy is the whole job. |
| **Unit maths that adds up** | Roughly ฿170–250 a unit for a named brand. A "full face" at ฿1,999 is either heavily diluted or a budget Korean toxin, which is fine if you are told so. |
| **A record of your dose** | A clinic that logs brand, units and injection points can reproduce a good result. One that does not, cannot. |
| **Willingness to under-treat** | A good injector starts conservative on a first visit and reviews at two weeks. You can always add more; you cannot take it out. |

## Is Botox good no matter where you get it?

No, and the variation is larger than most people expect. The toxin is standardised; the injector is not. Placement decides whether your brow lifts or drops, whether your smile stays symmetrical, and whether you look rested or frozen. Dose decides how long it lasts and how natural it looks.

There is also a supply question that does not arise with a machine-based treatment. Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine that must be stored cold and reconstituted correctly. Under-dosing by over-diluting a vial is the most common way a cheap price is made possible, and you cannot detect it except by the result fading in six weeks instead of three months.

## Why should a doctor perform Botox?

Because the risks are anatomical. Inject too low on the forehead and the brow drops; too close to the orbital rim and the eyelid can droop for weeks. Get the masseter wrong and chewing changes. Treat the trapezius without knowing the depth and you can weaken the shoulder. None of these are reversible; you wait for the toxin to wear off.

A doctor is also the person who should tell you that Botox is the wrong treatment when it is. Static lines that are visible at rest, hollowing, and skin laxity do not respond to toxin, and a clinic that sells you 40 units for a problem filler or resurfacing would fix is not making a clinical judgement.

## Alternatives and companions to Botox

| Treatment | What it does | When it beats Botox |
|---|---|---|
| **Dermal filler** | Adds volume | Static lines, hollow cheeks, undereye shadows, chin and jaw shape |
| **Skin boosters** (Profhilo, Rejuran) | Hydrate and improve quality | Crepey texture and dullness rather than movement lines |
| **Ulthera Prime / HIFU** | Lifts deep tissue | Sagging along the jawline and neck |
| **Thermage FLX** | Radiofrequency tightening | Loose, poor-quality skin |

Most good results in Bangkok are combinations. Toxin for movement, filler for volume, and a device for skin quality. If a clinic only ever recommends the one thing it is promoting that month, that tells you something.

## FAQ

### How much does Botox cost in Bangkok?

Bangkok clinics publish Botox at ฿170 to ฿250 per unit, or ฿7,500 to ฿29,800 for a 100-unit vial depending on the brand. Korean toxins such as Nabota, Neuronox and Aestox sit at the bottom of that range, and Allergan at the top. By area, published prices run from about ฿1,600 for bunny lines to ฿20,000 for calves. Ask for the brand and unit count, not just the area price.

### Which Botox brand is best in Bangkok?

Allergan has the longest track record and costs the most. Xeomin contains no complexing proteins, which makes it the usual choice for anyone who has stopped responding to another brand. Nabota is the mid-priced option and was the first Asian toxin to gain FDA approval. Korean toxins like Neuronox and Aestox work perfectly well and cost roughly half; the honest difference is track record, not effect.

### Is cheap Botox in Bangkok safe?

It depends entirely on why it is cheap. A budget Korean toxin, named openly and injected by a doctor, is legitimately cheaper. A cheap price achieved by over-diluting a vial or by using unnamed product is not, and you cannot tell by looking. Ask for the brand, ask for the unit count, and ask to see the sealed vial.

### How long does Botox last?

Three to four months for facial lines, usually longer for the masseter and often six months or more for excessive sweating. Your first treatment can wear off faster than later ones. If a treatment fades in under six weeks, the likely explanation is under-dosing rather than an unusual metabolism.

### Does Botox hurt?

Very little. The needle is fine and each injection takes a second, with a whole forehead treatment done in a few minutes. Clinics can apply numbing cream if you ask, though most people do not need it. Small bumps and occasional bruising settle within a day or two.
